\section{The Kiss of Peace}

\rubric{The greeting of the kiss of peace is usually ``Christ is in our midst'' with the
response ``He is and ever shall be.'' During Bright Season, the greeting is instead ``Christ
is risen'' with the response ``indeed He is risen.'' During the Nativity Season, the greeting
is instead ``Christ is born'' with the response ``glorify Him.''}

\speak{Priest}{Peace be to all.}

\speak{People}{And to thy spirit.}

\speak{Deacon}{Let us love one another that with one accord we may confess.}

\speak{Choir}{\cross~Father, Son, and Holy Spirit: the Trinity, one in essence and
undivided.}

\speak{Priest}{\uline{Christ is in our midst}.}

\speak{People}{\uline{He is and ever shall be}.}

\rubric{The people might make the same exchange with those near them.}
