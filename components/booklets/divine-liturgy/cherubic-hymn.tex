\section{The Cherubic Hymn}

\speak{Deacon}{Help us; save us; have mercy on us; and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.}

\speak{People}{Lord, have mercy.}

\speak{Priest}{That guarded always by Thy might we may ascribe \cross~glory unto Thee, to
the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.}

\subsect{The Cherubic Hymn}

\rubric{We may add additional settings of the Cherubic Hymn on a rotation.
    The words may differ slightly from the one below.}

\speak{Choir}{We who mystically represent (represent) the Cherubim and who sing to the
life-giving Trinity the thrice-holy hymn, let us now lay aside (lay aside) all earthly
care, let us now lay aside all earthly care.}
