\section{The Megalynarion}

\rubric{The hymn ``It is Truly Meet'' is usually sung here. During the Liturgy of St Basil,
this is replaced by the alternate hymn ``All of Creation''. During much of Bright Season,
this is replaced by the hymn ``The Angel Cried''. Sometimes something else is used. This
might be indicated in the bulletin.}

\speak{Priest}{Especially our all-holy, immaculate, most blessed and glorious Lady, the
Theotokos and ever-virgin Mary.}

\subsect{It is Truly Meet}

\speak{People}{It is truly meet; it is truly meet to call thee blessed, Lady Theotokos. Lady
ever greatly blessed and most perfect in innocence and the Mother of our God. Lady more
precious than the Cherubim and more glorious beyond all measure than the Seraphim, who
without corruption gavest birth to God the Word and art truly Theotokos, we magnify thee;
we magnify thee.}

\speak{Priest}{Among the first be mindful, O Lord, of our Metropolitan
SABA, whom do Thou grant unto Thy holy churches in
peace, safety, honor, health, and length of days, and rightly dividing the word of Thy
truth.}

\rubric{The following line is only done if a deacon is present.}

\speak{Deacon}{And of the people here present, of those whom they are remembering and of
all mankind.}

\speak{People}{And of all mankind.}

\speak{Priest}{Be mindful, O Lord, of this city in which we dwell and of every city and
countryside and of those who in faith dwell therein. Be mindful, O Lord, of those who
travel by sea, by land, and by air, the sick, the suffering, the captive, and their
salvation. Be mindful, O Lord, of those who bear fruit and do good works in Thy holy
churches and who remember the poor, and upon us all send forth Thy mercies.

And grant us with one mouth and one heart to glorify and praise Thine all-honorable and
majestic name, \cross~of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever
and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.}

\speak{Priest}{And may the mercies of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ be with you
all.}

\speak{People}{And with thy spirit.}
