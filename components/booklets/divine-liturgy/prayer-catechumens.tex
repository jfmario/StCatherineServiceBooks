\section{Prayer for the Catechumens}

\rubric{If catechumens are present, they are invited to the center of the church for this
prayer.}

\speak{Priest}{Let us pray to the Lord.}

\speak{People}{Lord, have mercy.}

\speak{Priest}{O Lord our God, Who dwellest on high and regardest things below, Who hast
sent forth as the salvation of the race of men Thine Only Begotten Son and God, our Lord
Jesus Christ: Look down upon Thy servants the catechumen(s) \textit{N.}\ who has/have
bowed his/her/their neck(s) before thee, make them worthy in due season of the laver of
regeneration, the remission of sins, and the robe of incorruption. Unite them to Thy holy,
catholic, and apostolic Church, and number them with Thine elect flock, that with us they
may glorify Thine all-honorable and majestic name, \cross~of the Father and of the Son and
of the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.}

\rubric{The catechumens return to their places.}
