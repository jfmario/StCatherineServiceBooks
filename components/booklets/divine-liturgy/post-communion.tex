\section{Post-Communion}

\rubric{The post-communion hymn is usually ``We Have Seen the True Light'' in Tone 2.
Sometimes, another hymn is sung instead. This might be indicated in the bulletin.}

\speak{Priest}{O God, save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance.}

\speak{People}{We have seen the true light. We have received the heavenly Spirit. We have
found the true faith, worshipping the undivided Trinity, Who has saved us.}

\speak{Priest}{Be Thou exalted, O God, above the heavens and Thy glory above all the
earth\ldots\ (3x)

\ldots Always now and ever and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.

Let our mouth be filled with Thy praise, O God, for Thou has counted us worthy to share Thy
holy, immortal, and spotless Mysteries. Keep us in sanctification that we may sing Thy
glory, meditating on Thy holiness all the day.

Alleluia. Alleluia. Alleluia.}

\speak{Deacon}{Stand upright. Having partaken of the divine, holy, immaculate, immortal,
heavenly, life-giving, and dread mysteries of Chirst, let us worthily give thanks unto the
Lord.}

\speak{People}{Lord, have mercy.}

\speak{Deacon}{Help us; save us; have mercy on us; and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.}

\speak{People}{Lord, have mercy.}

\speak{Deacon}{Asking that the whole day may be perfect, holy, peaceful, and sinless, let us
commend ourselves and each other and all our life unto Christ our God.}

\speak{People}{To Thee, O Lord.}

\speak{Priest}{We give thanks unto Thee, O Master Who lovest mankind, Benefactor of our
souls, for that Thou hast vouchsafed this day to feed us with Thy heavenly and immortal
Mysteries. Make straight our path, establish us all in Thy fear, guard our life, make firm
our steps, through the prayers and intercessions of the glorious Theotokos and ever-virgin
Mary and of all Thy saints.

For Thou art our sanctification, and unto Thee we ascribe \cross~glory, to the Father and
to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.}

\section{Prayer behind the Amvon}

\speak{Priest}{Let us go forth in peace.}

\speak{People}{In the Name of the Lord.}

\speak{Deacon}{Let us pray to the Lord.}

\speak{People}{Lord, have mercy.}

\speak{Priest}{O Lord, Who blesses those who bless Thee and sanctifies those who put their
trust in Thee, save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance, preserve the fullness of Thy
Church, sanctify those who love the beauty of Thy house, glorify them in recompense by Thy
divine power, and forsake us not who hope on Thee. Give peace to Thy world, to Thy churches,
to the priests, to the civil authorities, to the armed forces and to all Thy people.

For every good and perfect gift is from above and comes down from Thee, the Father of
lights, and unto Thee we ascribe \cross~glory, to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy
Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.}

\speak{People}{Amen.

Blessed be the Name of the Lord from henceforth and forevermore. (3x)}

\rubric{Note: If a special service is going to follow the Divine Liturgy, it often begins
here.}

\section{The Dismissal}

\speak{Priest}{\textit{Commemorates the saints\ldots}

\ldots whose memory we celebrate, and of all the saints, have mercy on us and save us,
forasmuch as He is good and loves mankind.

Through the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord Christ our God, have mercy
on us and save us.}

\speak{People}{Amen.}

\section{After the Dismissal}

\rubric{Young children may be dismissed for a brief Sunday School before announcements.}

\rubric{Announcements may be given.}

\rubric{When announcements are complete, the faithful line up for a final blessing. Each
person approaches the priest and reverences the cross, then departs to the left. They may
take the ``antidoron'' (remaining blessed bread), although priority should be given to
visitors and catechumens.}

\rubric{During this time, the choir will likely sing a hymn or two.}

\rubric{Afterwords, the service is complete, although post-communion prayers will be going
on in the altar. On
Sundays, we often have a time of food, drink, and fellowship following the service.}
