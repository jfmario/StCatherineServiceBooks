## Hymns of the Day

*Sing the appropriate hymns of the day. On weekdays, these are the hymns for the saint of the day or, if no saint is commemorated with hymns, for the day of the week. The usual weekday pattern follows.*

**Apolytikion of the Saint of the Day or of the Day of the Week**

*Note: There may be a festal or another hymn between "Glory" and "Both now." Otherwise, "Glory... Both now..." is chanted as one verse. Either way, the theotokion or other hymn follows "Amen."*

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit.

Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
