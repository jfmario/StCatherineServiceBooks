## O Gladsome Light

*Note: On weekdays, this is read rather than sung.*

Reader: O Gladsome Light of the holy glory of the immortal, heavenly, holy, blessed Father: O Jesus Christ! Now that we have come to the setting of the sun, and behold the light of evening, we praise God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. For meet it is at all times to worship Thee with voices of praise, O Son of God and Giver of Life; therefore all the world doth glorify Thee.
