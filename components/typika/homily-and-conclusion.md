## Homily and Conclusion

*A homily on the scriptural lessons or saint of the day may be delivered here.*

*Then, the faithful may say or sing the apolytikia (hymns) of the day.*

*After the leader makes any necessary announcements, he concludes the Typika, saying:*

Reader: Through the prayers of our holy fathers, O Lord Jesus Christ our God, have mercy on us, and save us.

People: Amen.
