## Prayer for Catechumens

Deacon/Priest: Let us pray to the Lord.

People/Choir: Lord, have mercy.

Priest: Unite them to Thy holy, catholic, and apostolic Church, and number them with Thine elect flock, that with us they may glorify Thine all-honorable and majestic name, of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

People/Choir: Amen.

## Prayer of the Cherubic Hymn

Deacon/Priest: Help us; save us; have mercy on us; and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.

People/Choir: Lord, have mercy.

Priest: That guarded always by Thy might we may ascribe glory unto Thee, to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

People/Choir: Amen.
