
## Post-Communion Litany

Deacon/Priest: Having partaken of the divine, holy, immaculate, immortal, heavenly, life-giving, and awesome mysteries of Chirst, let us worthily give thanks unto the Lord.

People/Choir: Lord, have mercy.

Deacon/Priest: Help us; save us; have mercy on us; and keep us, O God, by Thy grace.

People/Choir: Lord, have mercy.

Deacon/Priest: Asking that the whole day may be perfect, holy, peaceful, and sinless, let us commend ourselves and each other and all our life unto Christ our God.

People/Choir: To Thee, O Lord.

Priest: For Thou art our sanctification, and unto Thee we ascribe glory, to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

People/Choir: Amen.
