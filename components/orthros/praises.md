
## The Praises

*This is usually sung in a prescribed tone. Tone 4 is provided here on the following page. Sing together through "To Thee, O God, is due our song." Then intone the verses; an appointed stichera of the feast follows each one.*

Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord. Praise ye the Lord from the heavens; praise Him in the heights. To Thee, O God, is due our song.

Praise ye Him, all His angels; praise ye Him, all His hosts. To Thee, O God, is due our song.

1) Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him according to His excellent greatness.
2) Praise Him with the sound of the trumpet. Praise Him with the psaltery and harp.
3) Praise Him with the timbrel and dance; praise Him with stringed instruments and organs.
4) Praise Him upon the loud cymbals; praise Him upon the high-sounding cymbals. Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord.

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. Both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
