
## Before the Exaposteilarion

***Only on non-Pascha Sundays which are not Feasts of the Master. This is also done on Lazarus Saturday.***

Reader: Holy is the Lord our God.

Holy is the Lord our God.

Holy is the Lord our God.

Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship the footstool of His feat; for He is holy.

## Exaposteilarion and Theotokion

*Sing the appointed hymns, based on the rubrics. This comes from the Menaion if possible, otherwise it is based on the day of the week or, on Sundays, the tone of the week.*
