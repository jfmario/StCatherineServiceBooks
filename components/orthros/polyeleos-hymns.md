
## Polyeleos Hymns

*Sing the appointed Polyeleos hymns of the feast, based on appointed rubrics. The usual pattern follows:*

- The Polyeleos Hymn of the Feast

Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; both now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

- The Polyeleos Hymn of the Feast *(repeat)*
